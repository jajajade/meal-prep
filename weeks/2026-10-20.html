Cook-day prep schedule

Cook day 1 Tue 20 · ~45 min active

  1. 0:00 Oven to 210 °C fan. Chop the roast veg, toss with chickpeas, oil and cumin, and get the tray in (35 min).
  2. 0:08 Marinate the chicken. Start the mujadara onions.
  3. 0:15 Add rice, lentils and water to the mujadara; simmer 15 min. Put a pot of water on to boil.
  4. 0:18 Chicken into the air fryer (or on a second oven tray).
  5. 0:20 Boiling water: eggs in for 6½ min, then into an ice bath. Same water: blanch the greens for 2 min, then into ice.
  6. 0:30 Chop and mix the bean salad while everything cooks.
  7. 0:38 Stir together the garlic yoghurt. Rest the mujadara with the lid on.
  8. 0:45 Assemble dinner. Cool everything else, then pack into containers. Make tomorrow's oats.

Cook day 2 Thu 22 · ~30 min active

  1. 0:00 Oven to 210 °C fan. Cube the sweet potato and potato, toss with oil and salt, and roast 35–40 min.
  2. 0:05 Brown the mince with garlic and grated carrot.
  3. 0:12 Add passata, lentils, kidney beans and stock; simmer 15 min. Put the pasta water on.
  4. 0:20 Cook pasta shells for tonight and Sun only (cook fresh, or keep them separate).
  5. 0:30 Serve the mince pasta. Cool and pack the rest.

Lamb is pan-fried fresh on the night (Fri) and takes about 8 min. Thaw it in the fridge the night before.

Components

Nutrition is a rough estimate per serve. Quantities are for the whole batch.

Gochujang chicken (Pestle)

Tue · 4 serves · fridge 3 days (to Fri)

  • 520 g chicken thigh fillet, patted dry
  • 1½ tbsp gochujang
  • 1½ tsp sesame oil + 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp sugar (instead of brown sugar)
  • 3 garlic cloves, grated (instead of garlic powder)
  • ½ tsp salt, 1 tsp black pepper
  • Optional: 1 tsp baking powder for crispness
  1. Mix everything and coat the chicken.
  2. Air fry at 195 °C for 10 min, flip, then 6–10 min more until golden (or bake on a tray at 210 °C for about 22 min).
  3. Rest, then slice.
Est. per serve~220 kcal~27 g protein0 g fibre

Mujadara, quick version (Pestle)

Tue · 6 serves (~200 g) · fridge 4 days (to Sat)

  • 300 g rice, rinsed
  • 1 tin (420 g) lentils, drained
  • 2 onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp cumin, ½ tsp allspice, 1 tsp salt, pepper
  • 600 ml water
  1. Sauté the onion in oil with cumin and allspice for 6–8 min until soft.
  2. Add rice, lentils, salt and water. Bring to the boil, cover and simmer on low for 15 min.
  3. Rest 5 min with the lid on, then fluff. (Simplified from the original: no soaking, no fried-onion topping, no 30-min steam.)
Est. per serve~300 kcal~9 g protein~5 g fibre

Bean salad Jade's

Tue · 8 serves (~1 cup) · fridge 5 days (to Sun)

  • 1 tin each cannellini, kidney beans and lentils, drained and rinsed
  • 2 tomatoes, diced
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1 cucumber, diced
  • ½ red onion, finely diced
  • Handful of olives, sliced
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 tbsp red wine vinegar, salt, pepper
  1. Mix everything. It tastes better the next day.
Est. per serve~200 kcal~10 g protein~8 g fibre

Roast veg tray

Tue · 6 serves · fridge 4 days

  • 1 tin chickpeas, drained and patted dry
  • 500 g cup mushrooms, halved
  • 2 zucchini, in chunks (keep 1 for pasta)
  • 1 eggplant (~500 g), cubed
  • 3 tbsp olive oil, 2 tsp cumin, salt
  1. Toss everything on 1–2 lined trays without crowding.
  2. Roast at 210 °C fan for 30–35 min, turning once.
Est. per serve~150 kcal~6 g protein~6 g fibre

Blanched greens

Tue · 6 serves · fridge 4 days

  • 1 bunch broccolini
  • 2 bunches asparagus
  • 250 g green beans
  1. Trim. Boil in salted water for 2 min.
  2. Plunge into iced water, drain well, and store with paper towel.
Est. per serve~35 kcal~3 g protein~3 g fibre

Jammy eggs

Tue · 6 eggs · fridge 4 days, unpeeled

  1. Lower 6 fridge-cold eggs into boiling water for 6½ min.
  2. Ice bath for 5 min. Peel when you eat them.
Est. per egg~80 kcal~7 g protein0 g fibre

Garlic yoghurt

Tue · 6 serves (~50 g) · fridge 5 days

  • 300 g Greek yoghurt
  • 1 garlic clove, grated
  • Squeeze of lemon (or ½ tsp red wine vinegar), salt
  1. Stir together.
Est. per serve~50 kcal~5 g protein0 g fibre

One Pot Beef, Bean & Lentil Ragu (Pestle)

Thu · 6 serves (sauce only) · fridge 4 days (to Mon) or freeze

  • 500 g beef mince
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, 6 garlic cloves
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 1 tin lentils + 1 tin kidney beans, drained (instead of the original's cannellini in vodka sauce)
  • 700 g passata (instead of tomato paste + vodka sauce)
  • 250 ml stock
  • 1 tsp salt, chilli flakes, oregano
  • To finish: 120 g Greek yoghurt
  1. Brown the mince in oil with garlic and carrot for 6–7 min.
  2. Add passata, lentils, beans, stock and seasoning. Simmer 15 min until thick.
  3. Off the heat, stir in the yoghurt. Pasta is cooked separately rather than in the pot, so the sauce works for both the pasta and the loaded sweet potato.
Est. per serve~330 kcal~27 g protein~8 g fibre

Roast sweet potato & potato

Thu · 6 serves · fridge 4 days

  • 1 kg gold sweet potato
  • 600 g potatoes
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, salt, paprika
  1. Cut into 3 cm chunks (or halve the sweet potatoes for loading).
  2. Roast at 210 °C fan for 35–40 min.
Est. per serve~180 kcal~3 g protein~4 g fibre

Gochujang noodles

10 min

  • 75 g dry egg noodles
  • 1 serve gochujang chicken
  • ¼ bunch bok choy + handful of spinach
  • 1 tbsp soy, 1 tsp sesame oil, garlic, chilli
  • Optional: a jammy egg
  1. Cook the noodles; add the greens for the last minute.
  2. Drain, then toss with the sauce and chicken.
Est.~550 kcal (+80 with egg)~38 g protein (+7)~5 g fibre

Fried rice

10 min · uses day-old mujadara

  • 1½ serves mujadara
  • 1 egg (fresh)
  • ½ tin tuna or ¼ tin salmon
  • ¼ cup corn kernels + handful of spinach
  • 1 tsp oil, soy, spring onion
  1. Fry the corn in a hot pan, add the rice and fry 3 min.
  2. Push to the side and scramble the egg in the gap. Add the fish and spinach, season with soy.
Est.~720 kcal~40 g protein~10 g fibre

Lamb plate

12 min · lamb cooked fresh

  • 1 serve roast potatoes
  • 1 serve bean salad
  • 1 serve garlic yoghurt
  • Greens if any are left
  1. Season the chops; pan-fry in a hot pan for 3–4 min each side. Rest 3 min.
Est.~730 kcal~43 g protein~12 g fibre

Daily totals

A typical day is oats (~480) + lunch (~600–700) + dinner (~650–730) + snacks (~350–400: fruit, a glass of milk, extra bean salad or yoghurt). That comes to about 2150–2250 kcal, 105–115 g protein, 30–35 g fibre per person (estimate). Matteo: add ~50 kcal of extra rice, pasta or toast.

Protein comes from both animal and plant sources (chicken, beef, lamb, tuna, salmon, eggs, yoghurt, milk), and the plant proteins are paired to be complete: rice + lentils, beans + bread, pasta + lentils/beans. Veg across the week: broccolini, asparagus, green beans, mushrooms, zucchini, eggplant, bok choy, spinach, carrot, tomato, cucumber, sweet potato, potato and corn. The most perishable veg goes into Tuesday's prep.
